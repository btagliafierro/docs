Step 1 Geometry Definition
1. Geometry Definition
The user inputs the boundary geometric definition (XML surface description, STL mesh, or PLY CAD model) establishing the continuous shape volume.
2. Lattice Creation
GenCase overlays a uniform background 2D/3D grid (lattice) of candidate points spaced at inter-particle distance dp across the domain.
3. Point-in-Polygon Testing
A ray-casting / point-in-polyhedron algorithm evaluates each candidate point to test whether its coordinates lie strictly inside the defined shape.
4. Particle Selection & Pruning
All points outside the boundary are discarded. The surviving internal points are assigned SPH properties and exported as initial simulation particles.
